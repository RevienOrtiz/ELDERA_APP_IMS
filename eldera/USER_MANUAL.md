## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the **ELDERA Health App**, a dedicated mobile application designed for Senior Citizens. This app connects you to the ELDERA Information Management System (IMS), providing real-time updates on social pension, health services, and community events.

**Key Features:**
- **Accessibility First:** Large text options, high-contrast colors, and Text-to-Speech (voice) support.
- **Real-time Updates:** Instant notifications for pension payouts and health missions.
- **Event Tracking:** Integrated calendar and attendance history.

---

## 2. Getting Started
### System Requirements
- **Device:** Android Smartphone or Tablet.
- **Internet:** Requires Wi-Fi or Mobile Data for real-time updates.
- **Permissions:** 
  - **Notifications:** To receive important alerts.
  - **Storage:** To save your profile photo locally.

### Launching the App
Tap the **ELDERA** icon on your home screen. The app will launch and check for necessary configurations.

---

## 3. Login & Authentication
Upon opening the app, you will be greeted by the Login Screen.

### How to Login
1. **OSCA ID:** Enter your Senior Citizen ID number in the first field.
2. **Password:** Enter your password in the second field.
    - *Tip:* Tap the "Eye" icon to reveal or hide your password characters.
3. **Login Button:** Tap the **"LOGIN"** button to access your account.

**Note:** Once logged in, the app will remember you. You won't need to log in again unless you manually sign out.

---

## 4. Home Screen (Dashboard)
The Home Screen is your main hub for news and announcements.

### Announcement Feed
The center of the screen displays a list of the latest announcements. Each card shows:
- **Title:** The subject of the announcement.
- **Date & Time:** When the event or update is happening.
- **Details:** A brief description.

### Filtering Categories
You can filter announcements by tapping the buttons at the top:
- **ALL:** Shows every announcement.
- **PENSION:** Updates specifically about Social Pension payouts.
- **HEALTH:** Information on medical missions, check-ups, and vaccinations.
- **GENERAL:** Community news and general updates.
- **ID:** Updates regarding Senior ID claiming or renewal.

### Text-to-Speech (Hear the News)
- Look for the **Speaker Icon** on an announcement card.
- Tap it to have the app read the announcement aloud to you.
- Tap again to stop the audio.

---

## 5. Notifications
Access this screen by tapping the **"Bell"** icon in the bottom navigation bar.

- **Timeline:** View a history of all alerts you have received.
- **Color Codes:**
    - **Pink:** Health-related updates.
    - **Blue:** Pension-related updates.
    - **Green:** General news.
    - **Yellow:** ID claiming updates.
- **Status:** Unread notifications may be highlighted to catch your attention.

---

## 6. Schedule & Attendance
Access this screen by tapping the **"Calendar/Schedule"** icon in the bottom navigation bar. This screen has two tabs:

### Tab 1: Calendar
- Displays a monthly calendar view.
- **Dots on Dates:** Indicate scheduled events or announcements.
- **Interaction:** Tap any date to see the specific events for that day.

### Tab 2: Attendance
- **History:** View a log of events you have attended.
- **Statistics:** See a summary of your participation:
    - **Attended:** Number of events you were present for.
    - **Missed:** Number of events you missed.
    - **Total:** Total events relevant to you.

## 7. Settings & Customization
Access this screen by tapping the **"Gear"** icon in the bottom navigation bar.

### Appearance
- **Font Size:** Use the slider or buttons to make the text **Larger** or **Smaller** across the entire app. This helps improve readability.

### Language
- Toggle between **English** and **Filipino** (if available) to use the app in your preferred language.

### Account Security
- **Change Password:** 
    1. Enter your current password.
    2. Enter your new password.
    3. Confirm the new password.
    4. Tap "Save" to update.

### Logout
- Scroll to the bottom and tap **"Logout"** to sign out of your account securely.

---

## 8. Profile Management
From the Settings screen, you can view your **Profile**.

- **Personal Info:** View your registered Name, Age, Birthday, and Address.
- **Photo:** Your profile picture is synced with the OSCA office database.
- **Note:** You cannot edit your personal details (like Name or Birthday) directly in the app. Please visit the OSCA office to update your official records.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible Solution |
|-------|-------------------|
| **"Login Failed"** | Check your internet connection. Ensure your OSCA ID and Password are correct. |
| **"No Internet Connection"** | The app needs data to fetch new announcements. Connect to Wi-Fi or turn on Mobile Data. |
| **App is Silent (No Voice)** | Check your phone's volume. Ensure "Text-to-Speech" is enabled in your phone settings. |
| **Font is too small** | Go to **Settings** in the app and increase the Font Size. |

## 10. Step-by-Step Usage

### A. Login
- Open the app; the Login screen appears.
- Type your OSCA ID in the first field.
- Type your password in the second field.
- Tap the eye icon to show/hide password if needed.
- Tap LOGIN to proceed.
- If successful, you are redirected to the Home screen.

### B. Home Screen
- Review the announcements list in the center of the screen.
- Tap a category button at the top to filter:
  - ALL, PENSION, HEALTH, GENERAL, ID
- Tap an announcement to expand and read details (if available).
- Tap the speaker icon to play Text-to-Speech for that announcement.
- Tap the speaker icon again to stop reading.

### C. Notifications
- Tap the Bell icon in the bottom bar to open Notifications.
- Scroll through the timeline of alerts.
- Tap any notification to view full details.
- Opening a notification marks it as read (stored locally).
- Note the color codes:
  - Pink = Health, Blue = Pension, Green = General, Yellow = ID

### D. Schedule – Calendar
- Tap the Schedule icon in the bottom bar.
- Ensure the Calendar tab is active.
- Swipe left/right to move between months.
- Tap a date to view events for that day.
- If a date shows markers/dots, it has scheduled items.

### E. Schedule – Attendance
- In Schedule, switch to the Attendance tab.
- Review your attendance history list.
- Check the summary counts for Attended, Missed, and Total.
- Use available filter options to show “All”, “Attended”, or “Missed”.
- Tap an entry if a details view is available.

### F. Settings – Font Size
- Tap the Gear icon in the bottom bar to open Settings.
- Find Font Size controls.
- Increase or decrease the font size to your preference.
- Changes apply across all screens immediately.

### G. Settings – Language
- In Settings, find Language options.
- Choose your preferred language (e.g., English/Filipino).
- The app updates text labels throughout the UI.

### H. Settings – Change Password
- In Settings, open Change Password.
- Enter your current password.
- Enter your new password.
- Confirm the new password.
- Tap Save to update your password.

### I. Settings – Logout
- In Settings, scroll to the bottom.
- Tap Logout to securely sign out.
- You will return to the Login screen.

### J. Profile
- From Settings, navigate to Profile.
- View your Name, Age, Birthday, Address, and OSCA ID.
- Wait briefly for the profile photo to load from the server.
- To refresh, reopen Profile or pull-to-refresh (if available).
- For changes to official records, visit the OSCA office.

### K. Accessibility Tips
- If text is hard to read, increase Font Size in Settings.
- High-contrast colors are applied automatically by the app.
- Use the speaker icon on announcements for Text-to-Speech.

### L. Device Notifications (System)
- Ensure device notifications are enabled for ELDERA in your phone settings.
- Keep Wi-Fi or Mobile Data on to receive timely alerts.
